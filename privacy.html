Chessfall Ascension: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short: Chessfall Ascension has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your game progress stays on your device. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads.

This policy explains how the mobile game Chessfall Ascension (the "app"), made by Originsoft Consultancy ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the app on Android and to the Chessfall web challenges.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no sign-in and sends no data to us.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress (levels won, stars, hints, streaks, settings such as sound) on your device only. It never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded ads (for example, to earn a hint) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address (which can indicate approximate location),
  • device and app information (model, operating system, app version),
  • ad interactions and diagnostic information.

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Sharing

When you choose to share a result, the app opens your device's share sheet with a picture and text. Nothing is shared unless you pick an app and send it.

Web challenges

The Chessfall web challenges (challenges.originsoftconsultancy.com) are static pages. They use no cookies or analytics of their own. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and operation.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

Data sent by the ad service is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To remove the progress saved on your device, clear the app's data or uninstall it. For data processed by Google, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
